Serving throughput & LLM observability

Modern generative AI workloads split into two distinct platform requirements: high-throughput GPU inference and structured prompt/agent evaluation.

  • Ray Serve (Anyscale): Multi-node GPU orchestration and composable inference pipelines for vLLM and multimodal models.
  • BentoML: Pythonic model packaging with adaptive batching, OpenLLM support, and OCI container builds.
  • KServe: Standardized Kubernetes inference protocol supporting vLLM, Triton, and scale-to-zero Knative routing.
  • MLflow, W&B Weave & Comet Opik: Trace spans, latency/token cost telemetry, and regression evaluation suites for RAG and agents.
