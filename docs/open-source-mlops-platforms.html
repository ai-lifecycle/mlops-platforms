Choosing an open-source MLOps core

Open-source MLOps platforms generally fall into three architectural layers. Rather than adopting a monolithic suite on day one, most engineering teams pair one experiment tracker with one orchestrator.

  • MLflow (Apache-2.0): The de-facto open standard for experiment tracking, model packaging, Model Registry, and GenAI tracing.
  • DVC & Kedro (Apache-2.0): Best for Git-first workflows where datasets, pipeline DAGs, and model checkpoints live alongside application code.
  • ZenML & Metaflow (Apache-2.0): Portable Python pipeline frameworks that let you develop locally and deploy to AWS Batch, Airflow, or Kubernetes without rewriting step logic.
  • ClearML & Kubeflow (Apache-2.0): Full-stack self-hosted suites covering GPU scheduling, hyperparameter sweeps, and model serving.
