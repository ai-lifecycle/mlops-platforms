Why teams leave or pair SageMaker

SageMaker fits AWS-only shops, but teams needing local parity, multi-cloud GPUs, or transparent pricing pick alternatives.

Runs across AWS, Azure, and GCP with Delta Lake, Unity Catalog lineage, and managed MLflow.

ClearML & Valohai

Orchestrate training across on-prem GPUs and multiple clouds without rewriting pipelines to SageMaker SDKs.

Kubeflow & Ray

Open-source Kubernetes compute for distributed training and KServe or Ray Serve inference.

ZenML & Metaflow

Run steps locally on your laptop and target AWS Batch or Kubernetes without changing model code.
